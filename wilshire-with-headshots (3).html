The Beginning

She started
the same way you did.

Krista Mendyke did not begin her career with access to capital, a portfolio of properties, or a team of advisors. She began with a belief — that people in the final chapters of their lives deserve to live in a place that feels like home, led by people who genuinely care.

Her first facility was made possible by an investor who believed in her vision before she had proven it. That act of trust changed everything.

"I had the experience. I had the passion. What I needed was someone who would bet on me. When I found that, everything became possible."

Over the next 25 years, Krista built, operated, and scaled multiple assisted living communities across Wisconsin. She hired the caregivers, managed the surveys, handled the families, and navigated the regulations.

The Turning Point

What the industry
was becoming.

As the years passed, Krista watched a shift happen. The small, passionate owner-operators who had built remarkable communities began to disappear — not because they failed, but because the capital structures of the industry made it nearly impossible for them to compete and grow on their own terms.

Large investment groups acquired these communities. The culture often changed. What had been resident-first became investor-first.

"The people who built the best homes in this industry were being pushed out of it — not by failure, but by the absence of the right capital partner."

Krista knew there was another way. She had lived it from both sides. Wilshire was her answer to what the industry needed most.

The Journey

25 years of
building from within.

Every chapter of Krista's career contributed to the model Wilshire represents today. The operations. The exits. The lessons. The relationships. This is not a company that found senior housing as an asset class — it is a company born of a lifetime inside it.

The Early Years · 1999
First facility. First belief.
With the support of a capital partner who believed in her vision, Krista developed her first assisted living community in Wisconsin. The mission: a better place, a better life for everyone today.
The Growth Years · 2001–2024
Building a portfolio.
Krista scaled operations across countless Wisconsin communities, building deep experience in staffing, operations, resident care, regulatory compliance, and the full ownership lifecycle.
The Insight
Seeing what the industry was missing.
After two decades of operating, Krista recognized a structural gap: countless talented healthcare professionals who had the skill and passion to lead great communities but no path to owning one.
The Formation
Wilshire Group is born.
Wilshire was established to be the Platform Krista once needed, combining real estate capital, operating expertise, and a genuine belief in the people who deliver care.
Today
A platform in motion.
With our current communities and a growing vision for expansion, Wilshire is building the operator ownership platform the assisted living industry has always needed.

The Defining Step

Not a tenant forever.
A future owner.

Wilshire is built differently. Operators who meet defined performance benchmarks earn the right to advance, on their timeline, not ours.
